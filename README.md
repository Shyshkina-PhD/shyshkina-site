# Tetiana Shyshkina — Personal Academic Website

Personal website of Tetiana Shyshkina, historian and Digital Humanities researcher.

The site presents my research, selected projects, academic activities, Digital Humanities work, and photography.

## Research

My work focuses on Soviet Jewish history, historical networks, digitised archival sources, and computational methods.

The website includes information about:

- my doctoral research on the Jewish Anti-Fascist Committee;
- Yiddish-language sources and digital textual research;
- the TAMUZ digital census project;
- the Ukrainian Digital Humanities Initiative;
- selected talks, fellowships, and teaching activities;
- documentary and portrait photography.

## Methods and tools

My research combines historical interpretation with digital methods, including:

- Historical Network Analysis
- Python and data analysis
- NetworkX and pandas
- Transkribus / HTR
- text analysis
- TEI and corpus work
- Linked Open Data and SPARQL
- digital archives and GIS
