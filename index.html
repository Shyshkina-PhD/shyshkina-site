01 · Research

Networks, archives,
and Jewish history

My doctoral project examines the Jewish Anti-Fascist Committee between 1942 and 1948. I combine close reading with historical network analysis, prosopography, text analysis, and the computational exploration of digitised sources.

FocusSoviet Jewish history, wartime culture, transnational networks
MethodsHistorical network analysis, prosopography, text analysis, GIS
ToolsPython, NetworkX, pandas, Gephi, Transkribus, SPARQL
SourcesArchives, digitised newspapers, correspondence, institutional records

04 · Science communication

Ukrainian Digital Humanities Initiative

Making Ukrainian Digital Humanities more visible

I founded and run Ukrainian Digital Humanities Initiative, a bilingual science communication project on Instagram.

The initiative presents Ukrainian Digital Humanities projects, researchers, digital archives, databases, corpora, maps, research infrastructures, computational methods, and academic opportunities to a wider audience. It also helps connect Ukrainian work with international Digital Humanities communities.

Posts are built around concrete examples: one method, project, source, or research case at a time.

07 · About

Tetiana Shyshkina

I am a historian and Digital Humanities researcher from Kyiv, currently based in Berlin.

I am completing a PhD in History at Justus Liebig University Giessen and the International Graduate Centre for the Study of Culture. My work focuses on Soviet Jewish history, historical networks, digitised archives, and computational research methods.

My academic background spans history, Jewish studies, political science, and software engineering. This combination shapes my interest in research infrastructures that keep historical interpretation closely connected to data, sources, and their limits.
